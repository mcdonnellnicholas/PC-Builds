All Black RGB PC w/ Ryzen 5 4500, GTX 1660 Super 6GB





This build was made in late 2024 as a budget gaming PC from a budget of $500. With a GTX 1660 Super with 6GB of GDDR6 memory and a base clock speed of 1530 MHz can produce 336.0 GB/s of bandwidth, it is an excellent workstation allowing up to 4 monitors (2x4K resolution and 2x1440p resolution) monitors of use simultaneously and even capable of running most AAA games at 1080p high settings with good framerates. Due to the limited VRAM and base clock speed, this is not recommended for 1440p+ resolution gaming. The parts list is as follows:

  • Ryzen 5 4500 6-core, 12-thread processor
  • GTX 1660 Super 6GB VRAM graphics card
  • 16GB DDR4 3200Mhz XPG RGB
  • ASrock B450M-HDV R4.0 AM4 Socket
  • Apevia SPIRIT600W Spirit 600W ATX PSU
  • EDUP PCI-E WiFi 6 Network Card
  • Cooler Master MasterBox Q300L Micro-ATX Tower
  • Thermalright TL-C12C-S X3 CPU Fans + ARGB fan hub

Senior Final Project: Serpent Cipher


This project is a high-performance Python implementation of the Serpent block cipher, a finalist for the AES standard. It utilizes bit-slice processing to maximize throughput across 32-bit registers.


  • Bit-Slice Processing: Efficient simultaneous application of 4-bit S-boxes.
  • 32-Round Logic: Full implementation of encryption and decryption transformations.
  • Key Schedule: 256-bit expansion system based on the golden ratio Phi.
  • Padding: Support for variable-length messages via PKCS#7.
